If p and q are both odd, the sum is even and bigger than 2, so one of them is 2. Let p = 2. q = 2 gives 8, not prime. q = 3 gives 17, prime. For larger primes q, we know q^2 is 1 mod 3 and 2^q is 2 mod 3, so 2^q + q^2 is divisible by 3 and bigger than 3. So the answer is (2, 3) and (3, 2).
