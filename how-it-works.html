How it works

Two things are worth knowing before you start: how hard each paper is, and which papers are free. Almost everything is.

The difficulty scale

Every question on the site has a difficulty rating from 4.5, the most accessible, to 9.0, the hardest question in the collection. The scale compares the questions here with each other. It is not an official exam scale.

Solid4.5 to 5.9Hard6.0 to 7.4Severe7.5 to 9.0

Papers use the same scale

A paper's rating is the average of two numbers: the mean rating of its twenty questions, and the mean rating of its five hardest. A paper full of steady questions and a paper with a few brutal ones can therefore both rate highly. For example, Set B Paper 1 has a question average of 6.5 and a hardest-five average of 8.0, so it rates 7.3.

Paper cards also show the single hardest question, so you know what the worst moment of the paper will feel like.

Free and locked papers

  • Free, no account
    Almost everything. TMUA Sets A to D with solutions, the ESAT mocks and ESAT Drill, every free paper in Drill, the question bank, Arena, duels, the problem of the week, the geometry tools, and every olympiad paper and solution, including the UKMT SMC 2026 solutions.
  • Free account
    Optional. An account syncs your progress across devices and puts you on leaderboards. You need one to redeem a code or spend coins, because unlocks are saved to your account. Create an account.
  • Locked
    TMUA Sets E onwards. Unlock a paper with a code won in a challenge, or with coins in the Shop (every locked set except J, which is codes only). Once unlocked, a paper stays unlocked on every device you sign in on.
  • Earned with coins
    The book and extras. Chapter 1 and the complete Beyond the Incenter, titles, themes, streak freezes and the Labs minigames, all in the Shop.

Where to win a code

Challenges are posted on Instagram at @kevinvariant. A code unlocks one paper, or occasionally every locked paper. Some codes can be claimed only once, or only by the first few people, so redeem yours promptly on the unlock page.

Earning coins

Every question you solve earns coins, and harder questions earn more: 2 coins below a rating of 6.0, 4 from 6.0, 7 from 7.0 and 12 from 8.0, up to 400 coins a day. The Rewards page shows your level, streak, quests and badges. The Shop is where you spend coins.

Your data

Without an account, your scores, streaks and progress are stored only in this browser. Clearing the site's data removes them. If you sign in, your progress syncs to your account so it follows you between devices. Scores appear on Results only when you choose to log them, and logged scores are anonymous and pooled. If you choose to, your answers can also be shared anonymously to help rate the questions; see the privacy note.
