Privacy

If you agree, Kevinvariant shares your answers anonymously so the questions can be rated fairly. You can practise either way. This page explains exactly what that means, and what the site's other features send.

What is shared, and why

Smart Practice picks questions at the right level for you. To do that well it needs to know how hard each question really is, and the only fair way to find out is from how real students answer them. So, if you choose Share anonymously, each answer you give from then on sends these items and nothing else:

A random code
Made by your browser, for example 3f9c…. It is not your name, email or account, and it is different on each device.
The question
Its id, such as B1-7.
The option you picked
A letter, such as C, and whether it was right.
How long you took
In whole seconds.
When you answered
The date and time.
Where on the site
For example Smart Practice, Drill or the question bank.

Answers you gave before choosing, or while sharing is off, never leave your browser.

What sharing never collects

No names, emails, school, age, location or free text, and no link to your Kevinvariant account. Shared answers are sent without your sign-in details, so even if you are signed in they cannot be matched to your account. Sharing never stores IP addresses. The site uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

Like every website, the services that host Kevinvariant (GitHub Pages for the pages, Supabase for the database) keep short-lived technical logs of requests, which include IP addresses, for security and reliability. We do not use them.

Who sees it

Nobody can read the shared answers from the website, including other visitors. Once a week a calibration program works out how hard each question is from all the shared answers together, and only those results (a difficulty number for each question) are published. Individual answers are never published.

The shared answers are kept while they help rate the questions. Your scores, streaks and progress stay in this browser, or in your account if you sign in to sync them; that is separate from sharing and is described on How it works.

Switching off and deleting

Choose Keep on this device above at any time to stop sharing; you can still use everything on the site. Delete what this browser shared removes every answer sent with this browser's random code and gives it a new code. Because the code is random and kept only in this browser, we cannot find your answers any other way, so delete them from each device you used.

Accounts and other features

These are separate from anonymous sharing, and each is optional.

Accounts
If you create an account, your email address is used to sign you in, and your progress, coins and unlocks are saved to it so they follow you between devices. Your email is never shown to other visitors.
Leaderboard
Only if you switch it on: your display name, title and points appear on the leaderboard. If you do not choose a name, you are listed as “Player” and four digits. No part of your email address is ever shown.
Results
If you log a paper score, only the paper and the score are added to an anonymous tally. To stop spam, a counter tied to your internet connection's IP address is kept for about a day, then deleted automatically.
Mock interviews
What you type is sent to an AI model (run by Cloudflare, or another AI provider) to write the interviewer's replies. We do not keep it, but do not type personal details. A similar day-long counter tied to your IP address limits how many interviews each connection can start.
