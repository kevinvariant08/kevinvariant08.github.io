Privacy

What stays in your browser

Without an account, everything you do on the site is saved in your browser's local storage on this device and nowhere else. That includes your Pathways progress, Drill and Arena results, scores, coins, streaks, badges, review queue, problem of the week answers, your dark mode and theme choices, and the history of solutions you have had marked. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes all of it.

If you install the app or visit often, the browser also keeps copies of pages and papers you have opened so they work offline. The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Anonymous answer sharing (optional)

Smart Practice needs to know how hard each TMUA question really is, and the fairest way to find out is from how students answer them. If you agree, your answers are shared anonymously for that purpose. You can practise either way.

Sharing on this browser is not chosen yet.

What each shared answer contains

A random code
Made by your browser, for example 3f9c…. It is not your name, email or account, and it is different on each device.
The question
Its id, such as B1-7.
The option you picked
A letter, such as C, and whether it was right.
How long you took
In whole seconds.
When you answered
The date and time.
Where on the site
For example Smart Practice, Drill or the question bank.

Nothing else is sent: no name, email, school, age, location or free text. Answers you gave before choosing, or while sharing is off, never leave your browser. Shared answers are sent without your sign-in details, so even if you are signed in they cannot be matched to your account, and the sharing system does not store IP addresses.

Nobody can read the shared answers through the website, other visitors included. Once a week a calibration program works out a difficulty number for each question from all the shared answers together, and only those numbers are published. The answers are kept while they help rate the questions. Delete what this browser shared removes every answer sent with this browser's code and gives it a new one; since the code exists only in your browser, do this on each device you used.

Accounts (optional)

You never need an account to use the lessons, papers or practice. If you create one, it is handled by Supabase, the database service the site uses.

Sign-in details
Your email address and password, and a display name if you give one. Supabase stores the password hashed; I cannot see it. Supabase sends the confirmation and password reset emails. Your browser keeps a sign-in token in local storage so you stay signed in.
Synced progress
The progress listed above (Pathways, Drill, Arena, scores, rewards, duels, problem of the week, review queue and traps) is copied to your account so it follows you between devices. Proof marker history and API keys are not synced.
Coins and unlocks
Codes you redeem, papers you unlock and things you buy with coins in the Shop are saved to your account.
Leaderboard
Only if you switch it on: your display name, title, points, level and streak appear on the leaderboard. Without a display name you are listed as “Player” and four digits. No part of your email address is shown.
Labs high scores
If you are signed in when you finish a Labs minigame, your score and display name are added to that game's public high-score table.
Problem of the week
If you post a method in the discussion, the post and your display name are shown to other visitors who open that week's discussion.

Your email address is never shown to other visitors and is used only for signing in and account emails.

Proof marker, mock interviews and results

These features send what you type to an AI model, so do not type personal details into them.

Proof marker: free marker
The problem and your written solution go to the site's own server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes them to an AI model run by Cloudflare Workers AI and returns the mark. The worker does not save your text. To keep the free service within its limits it counts requests from each IP address for about a day; the counter then expires.
Proof marker: your own API key
Your browser sends the problem and solution straight to the provider you choose (OpenAI, Google Gemini or Anthropic), using your key, under that provider's terms. The key and the text never pass through Kevinvariant. If you tick Remember the key in this browser, the key is saved in this browser's local storage; untick it to clear it.
Proof marker: any chatbot
You copy a prompt into a chatbot yourself and paste its reply back. The site sends nothing.
Mock interviews
What you type goes through the same Cloudflare Worker to an AI model, which writes the interviewer's replies. The worker does not save the conversation, and the same day-long IP counter limits how many interviews each connection can start.
Results
If you log a paper score, only the paper and the score are added to an anonymous tally. A day-long IP counter stops one connection logging too many scores.

Services the site uses

Each of these receives your IP address and browser details when your browser contacts it, as any web server does, and keeps short-lived technical logs under its own privacy policy. I do not use those logs.

GitHub Pages
Hosts the pages and PDFs.
Supabase
Accounts, synced progress, unlocks, the leaderboard, discussion posts and anonymous answer sharing.
Cloudflare
Runs the worker behind the free proof marker, the mock interviews and the results tally.
Google Fonts
Supplies the two typefaces used on every page.
jsDelivr
Supplies MathJax, which draws the mathematics, and the Supabase sign-in library.

Links to Amazon, Instagram, UKMT, the AoPS wiki and other sites only contact those sites when you click them.

Deleting your data

On this device: clear this site's data in your browser settings. Shared answers: use Delete what this browser shared above, on each device. Synced progress: sign in and use Delete my synced progress on your account page. The whole account: message @kevinvariant on Instagram and say which email address you signed up with; the account and everything stored with it will be deleted. Leaderboard: switching it off on the Rewards page removes your entry.
