# Errata: Beyond the Incenter

Mathematics is a collaborative process. While every effort has been made to ensure the mathematical and structural accuracy of this monograph, first editions inevitably contain minor (or even major) errors. 

If you spot any typos, logical gaps, or misaligned `TikZ` diagrams, please submit them by opening an Issue in this repository. 

The physical manuscript is regularly updated based on community feedback. Thank you to everyone who contributes to refining this resource.
