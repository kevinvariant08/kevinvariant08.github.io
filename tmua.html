How to use these papers

A good routine: sit a paper timed in Drill, mark it, and log your score on Results. Every question you miss goes into Review mistakes, which brings it back after 1, 3 and 7 days. Use the Technique index to drill the methods you keep missing. Not sure which set to start with? See where to start.

Practice tools

Smart Practice

Questions chosen to suit your level as it changes, then an estimate of your level, your strongest and weakest topics, and what to practise next.

Drill

Full papers under exam conditions, technique drills with a solution after every answer, and a weakest-first queue built from your mistakes.

Question bank

All 120 questions from the free Sets B to D, ranked from 4.5 to 9.0, filterable by paper and technique, each playable on the spot.

Technique index

Every question filed by the idea that cracks it: telescoping, invariants, the inverse-function rectangle, and the rest.

Review mistakes

Every question you get wrong comes back after 1 day, then 3, then 7. Clear the 7-day review and it is mastered.

Results

Log a paper score anonymously and see how it compares with everyone else who has sat the same paper.

Mock interviews

Admissions interview practice: twenty-five minutes, one original problem in parts you can't see in advance, a nudge button that counts against you, and a locked debrief.
