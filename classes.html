What your teacher will see. A first name and initial is enough.

Your teacher will see your name in this class, the Pathways units you have finished, your level-check scores and when you were last active. Nothing else, and never your email. You can leave a class at any time. More in the privacy page.

Teach a class

Make a class, read the code out, and set units from the Pathways course or a level check. Students need a free account, so that their progress can reach you. A class holds up to 60 students, and you can have 10 active classes.
