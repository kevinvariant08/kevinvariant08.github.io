Mock interview simulators

Oxbridge maths interviews are twenty-five minutes with one problem, delivered in parts you cannot see in advance, with a tutor who probes every step and gives ground only when you ask. These simulators follow the same format. Each problem is original, starts with a part most candidates can do and ends with one few can, and comes with a mark scheme, a scored debrief and sample performances scoring 0 and 10, all locked until you finish.

Interviewer

Free interviews are paid for by this site and capped each day so the budget lasts. If you hit the cap, switch to self-guided or use your own key.

Whichever mode you pick, the interviewer never gives answers. It asks you to justify what you claim, gives the smallest possible nudge when you ask for one, and moves you on only when you have earned it.
