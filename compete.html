Practise and compete

Short daily practice on the same questions as the TMUA papers, with a race, a rival or a streak to keep you coming back. A little every day adds up.

  • Compete
  • Rewards and progress

Compete

Arena

Race a bot to the right answer. Pick an opponent, from a slow warm-up bot to one that answers like an olympian, or set its speed yourself. Best of five, with a rating that moves as you win. There's also a ten-minute ladder that climbs in difficulty until you run out of lives.

Friend duel

Play five questions and send the link. Your friend races your ghost on the same five, and each question goes to the faster correct answer.

Problem of the week

One hard problem, the same for everyone. The discussion of methods unlocks only after you have committed an answer.

Kyuclid

Contest problems ranked like martial-arts belts, from 8 kyu warm-ups to 1 kyu IMO proofs. Answers are checked instantly, proofs are marked against a rubric, and you can duel a ghost on the same problem. A separate site, still a first draft.

Rewards and progress

Every question you solve earns coins, and harder questions earn more. How coins and unlocks work.

Rewards

Level up from Point to Invariant, keep a daily streak alive, take on the daily challenge everyone gets, clear three weekly quests, collect 44 badges across four tiers and unlock themes that re-colour the whole site.

Shop

Spend coins on titles, streak freezes, themes and maths minigames. Every locked TMUA set except J can be unlocked here too (Sets E to I, K and L), and the vault holds a chapter of Beyond the Incenter, then the whole book, for the most dedicated.

Labs

Maths minigames you unlock in the Shop.

My progress

Accuracy by technique, how long questions take you, your interview trend and a twelve-week activity map. Stored only in your browser unless you sign in.
