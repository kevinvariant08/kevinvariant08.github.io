TMUA topic notes

7 sets of notes, each with worked examples, harder questions and seventeen practice problems with full solutions.

Mathematical knowledge, for both papers, in specification order

Quadratics, polynomials and inequalities

FreeNotes

Indices and surds, completing the square, the discriminant, inequalities with unknown signs, equations that reduce to quadratics, the factor and remainder theorems, roots and coefficients, and where the roots of a quadratic lie, with seventeen practice problems. Specification MM1.

Notes (PDF)

Sequences, series and the binomial expansion

FreeNotes

Recurrences and periodic sequences, arithmetic and geometric series, sums to infinity with a parameter, sigma notation and telescoping sums, and single coefficients in binomial products, with seventeen practice problems. Specification MM2.

Notes (PDF)

Trigonometry

FreeNotes

Exact values and the unit circle, the two identities, equations on an interval and all their solutions, ranges of trigonometric expressions, the ambiguous case, segments and sectors, and three-dimensional problems, with seventeen practice problems. Specification MM4.

Notes (PDF)

Exponentials and logarithms

FreeNotes

The graphs of exponentials and logarithms, the laws and the change of base, equations and inequalities with domain checks, comparing sizes without a calculator, counting digits with log 2, and counting solutions as a parameter varies, with seventeen practice problems. Specification MM5.

Notes (PDF)

Tangents, integration and area

FreeNotes

Differentiating powers, tangents and normals, integrals against areas, the Fundamental Theorem in both forms, transformed integrals, the inverse function rectangle and the trapezium rule, with seventeen practice problems. Specification MM6 and MM7.

Notes (PDF)

Graphs and sketching

FreeNotes

Derivatives and the nature of stationary points, asymptotes, transformations, trigonometric graphs, modulus, counting solutions, the cubic test, and seventeen practice problems. Specification MM8.

Notes (PDF)

Logic and proof, for Paper 2

Logic for Paper 2

FreeNotes

And, or and not, implication and the forms of “if”, converse and contrapositive, necessary and sufficient conditions, quantifiers and their order, and negating statements, with seventeen practice problems. Specification Arg1 to Arg4.

Notes (PDF)
