Conjecture sandbox

Build a configuration, drag it about, and let the page tell you what stays true. Every relation it spots is re-tested against random perturbations of the free points, so it can tell a theorem from a coincidence. Export the finished figure as tkz-euclide code that reconstructs every derived point rather than hard-coding it.
