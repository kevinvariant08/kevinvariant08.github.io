Competition maths, in order

Five levels, from the JMC and AMC 8 up to BMO2 and the USAMO: 42 units so far. Each unit is a lesson on one topic, six original problems with hints and full solutions, and a list of real past contest problems on that topic in order of difficulty. Everything is free, and your progress is saved as you work.

Where should I start?

Pick the next competition you are preparing for. If it already feels comfortable, start one level higher.

The levels

Units within a level can be taken in any order, though each lists what it builds on. Strategy units are worth doing just before the contest.

How a unit works

  1. Lesson. One topic, with the theorems stated and proved and three or four worked examples. The examples show the first attempts that fail as well as the one that works.
  2. Practice. Six original problems rated from one to four stars, each with a hint and a full solution. You mark each one as “got it” or “not yet”. Try one from Level 3 on the home page.
  3. Contest ladder. Eight to twelve real UKMT, BMO, AMC and AIME problems on the same topic, easiest first, each linked to the official paper or the AoPS wiki rather than copied here.
